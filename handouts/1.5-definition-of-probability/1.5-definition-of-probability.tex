\prob Suppose that a fair, six-sided die is rolled. Construct a probability measure $P$, including a description of the set of events.

\vfill
\prob Explain why $P(\emptyset) = 0$.

\vfill
\prob Explain why $P(A^c) = 1 - P(A)$, for all events $A$.

\vfill
\prob Explain why $P(A) \leq P(B)$, if $A$ and $B$ are events such that $A\subset B$.

\vfill
\newpage
\prob Explain why $0\leq P(A) \leq 1$ for all events $A$.

\vfill
\prob Explain why $P(A\cup B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A\cap B)$ for all events $A$ and $B$.

\vfill
\prob A patient arrives at a doctor’s office with a sore throat and low-grade fever. After an exam, the doctor decides that the patient has either a bacterial infection or a viral infection or both. The doctor decides that there is a probability of $0.7$ that the patient has a bacterial infection and a probability of $0.4$ that the person has a viral infection. What is the probability that the patient has both infections?

\vfill
\newpage
\prob A contractor is building an office complex and needs to plan for water and electricity demand. After consulting with prospective tenants and examining historical data, the contractor decides that the demand for electricity will range somewhere between $1$ and $150$ (in millions of kilowatt-hours per day) and water demand will be between $4$ and $200$ (in thousands
of gallons per day). All combinations of electrical and water demand are considered possible.

\medskip
\begin{enumerate}
\item Construct an appropriate sample space for all combinations of electrical and water demand.\vfill

\item Construct an appropriate probability measure.\vfill

\item The ``high demand'' event is when the water demand is at least $100$ or the electricity demand is at least $115$. What is the probability of this event?\vfill
\end{enumerate}
