\prob Suppose that a committee composed of eight people is to be selected from a group of $20$ people.

\medskip
\begin{enumerate}
\item How many different groups of people are there that might be on the committee?\vfill
\item If each of the eight people on the committee is to be assigned one of eight different jobs, how many ways are there to choose the committee of eight people and assign them jobs?
\end{enumerate}

\vfill
\prob Suppose that a committee of $12$ people is selected in a random manner from a group of $100$ people. Determine the probability that two particular people and will both be selected.

\vfill
\newpage
\prob Suppose that a fair coin is tossed 10 times.

\medskip
\begin{enumerate}
\item What is the probability of obtaining exactly three heads?\vfill
\item What is the probability of obtaining three or fewer heads?\vfill
\end{enumerate}
