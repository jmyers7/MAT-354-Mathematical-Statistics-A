\prob (Example 2.1.9) Two boxes contain long bolts and short bolts. Suppose that one box contains $60$ long bolts and $40$ short bolts, and that the other box contains $10$ long bolts and $20$ short bolts. Suppose also that one box is selected at random and a bolt is then selected at random from that box. What is the probability that the bolt is long?
