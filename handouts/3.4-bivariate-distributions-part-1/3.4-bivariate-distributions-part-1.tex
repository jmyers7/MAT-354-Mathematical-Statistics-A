\prob Let a coin be flipped two times with a probability $p=0.75$ of obtaining heads. Let $X$ be the number of heads obtained on the first flip, and $Y$ the total number of heads obtained on both flips. Describe the joint probability distribution of $X$ and $Y$.

\vfill
\prob A fair coin is flipped three times. One of the variables of interest is $Y$, the total number of heads obtained on the three flips. Let $X$ denote the amount of money won on a side bet	 in the following manner. If the first head occurs on the first toss, you win $\$1$. If the first head occurs on flip $2$ or flip $3$ you win $\$2$ or $\$3$, respectively. If no head appears, you lose $\$1$ (that is, win $-\$1$.)

\medskip
\begin{enumerate}
\item Find the joint probability function for $X$ and $Y$.\vfill
\item What is the probability that you will win $\$1$ or less and fewer than three heads will occur?\vfill
\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\prob (Example 3.4.7) Suppose that $X$ and $Y$ are jointly continuous with density function
	\[
	f(x,y) = \begin{cases}
	cx^2 y & : x^2 \leq y \leq 1, \\
	0 & : \text{otherwise},
	\end{cases}
	\]
for some constant $c$.

\medskip
\begin{enumerate}
\item Compute $c$.\vfill
\item Compute $P(X\geq Y)$.\vfill
\end{enumerate}
