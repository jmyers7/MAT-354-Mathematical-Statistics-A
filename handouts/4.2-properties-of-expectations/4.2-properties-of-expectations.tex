\prob Compute the expectation of a random variable $X\sim \mathcal{B}in(n,p)$.
\vfill

\prob (Example 4.2.6) Suppose that a person types $n$ letters, types the addresses on $n$ envelopes, and then places each letter in an envelope in a random manner. Let $X$ be the number of letters that are placed in the correct envelopes. Compute $E(X)$.
\vfill

\prob Suppose that $X_1$, $X_2$, and $X_3$ form a random sample from the continuous uniform distribution on $[0,1]$. Determine the value of
\[
	E\left[ (X_1 - 2X_2 +X_3)^2 \right].
\]
