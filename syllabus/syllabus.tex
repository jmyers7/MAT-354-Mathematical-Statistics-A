\textbf{Components.} 

\smallskip
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Quizzes.} There will be short weekly quizzes given on Fridays. Your two lowest quiz scores will be dropped at the end of the semester. \smallskip
\item \textbf{Midterm exams.} We will have two midterm exams, roughly evenly spaced throughout the semester. \smallskip
\item \textbf{Final exam.} There will be a comprehensive final exam at the end of the semester. Your final exam percentage will replace your lowest midterm exam percentage (if greater).
\end{itemize}
\smallskip

Each component of the class will be assigned the following weights to compute your final grade.

\smallskip
\begin{center}
\begin{minipage}{0.85\linewidth}
Quizzes \dotfill 30\% \\
Midterm exams \dotfill 35\% \\
Final exam \dotfill 35\%
\end{minipage}
\end{center}

\smallskip
Final letter grades are assigned using the following cutoffs: A is $\geq 93\%$, A- is $\geq 90\%$, B+ is $\geq 87\%$, B is $\geq 83\%$, B- is $\geq 80\%$, C+ is $\geq 77\%$, C is $\geq 73\%$, C- is $\geq 70\%$, D+ is $\geq 67\%$, D is $\geq 64\%$, D- is $\geq 60\%$. Any percentage less than $60\%$ is an E.
